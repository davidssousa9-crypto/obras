Our story — 01

[ 01 / 06 ]

One rule carries every job we take.

Metric started in 2012 with one excavator and a driver who refused to cut corners. The rule hasn't changed: plan like the deadline matters, build like the structure outlives us — on every site, in every province.

Milestones — 05

[ 05 / 06 ]
  1. 2012

    One excavator

    Metric starts as a two-person site-prep crew in the Lower Mainland — one excavator, one pickup truck and a rule about corners.

  2. 2015

    First municipal package

    The first deep-utilities contract for a BC municipality finishes early. The phone starts ringing and doesn't stop.

  3. 2018

    Machines that measure

    Machine control, drone topo and live as-builts become standard on every site. Survey error stops reaching the ground.

  4. 2021

    East of the Rockies

    A second office opens in Scarborough, Ontario, and a third in Calgary. Same crews, same standard — three time zones.

  5. 2024

    Five million cubic metres

    Cumulative earth moved clears five million cubic metres — enough to fill nearly two thousand Olympic pools.

  6. 2025

    Northgate Interchange

    1.2 million cubic metres moved under the biggest single contract in company history — zero lost-time incidents, perfect as-builts.
