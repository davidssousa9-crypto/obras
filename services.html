What we do

What the ground needs, we build.

← Home Est. 2012 — Vancouver · Toronto · Calgary

Four trades. One set of standards.

Whatever the ground asks for, Metric answers with the same crews, the same plant and the same rule — plan like the deadline matters, build like the structure outlives us.

Earthworks — 01

[ 01 / 05 ]

Great ground starts with a plan.

Mass excavation, site grading and structural backfill at any scale — moved, placed and compacted to spec, with survey proof at every lift.

  • 01

    Mass excavation

    Bulk dig run with in-house plant and machine control, sequenced to hit grade the first time.

  • 02

    Site grading

    Building pads, road subgrades and drainage falls levelled to design tolerance — not eyeball.

  • 03

    Structural backfill

    Select fill placed and compacted in lifts, tested and documented before it's covered.

  • 04

    Cut & fill balance

    Earthwork volumes computed up front, so imported or exported dirt stays off the invoice.

Utilities — 02

[ 02 / 05 ]

Installed once, proven before cover.

Water, storm and sanitary mains, deep utilities and trenchless crossings — installed and proven before a single yard of cover goes back.

  • 01

    Deep utilities

    Mains down to eight metres, shored and dewatered, installed to municipal standard.

  • 02

    Trenchless crossings

    HDD and auger bores that keep roads, rail and rivers open while the utility goes under.

  • 03

    Storm & sanitary

    Network sewers and storm systems sized, set and verified for first flush.

  • 04

    Testing & as-builts

    Hydrostatic and deflection testing, with live as-builts handed over before cover.

  • 0km Mains installed
  • 0 Trenchless crossings
  • 0% Lines tested before cover

Roadworks — 03

[ 03 / 05 ]

From subgrade to final surface.

Full-depth road construction, paving and rehabilitation — from subgrade to final surface and everything between. We build roads that take the first truck and the ten-thousandth the same way.

  • 01

    Full-depth reconstruction

    Existing pavement out, subgrade reworked, base placed — built again from the ground up.

  • 02

    Asphalt paving

    Stringless screed and paired rollers that hold line and level across the whole mat.

  • 03

    Concrete paving

    Mainline and intersection slabs placed, finished and cured to specification.

  • 04

    Rehabilitation

    Mill and fill, rubblizing and surface treatments that extend the life of the asset.

  • 0K+ Tonnes of asphalt laid
  • 0% Smoothness measured at spec

Remediation, erosion control and habitat restoration done to standard — with the documentation that follows. We prove the ground is better on paper, too.

How we work — 05

[ 05 / 05 ]
  1. 01

    Survey & assess

    Ground, survey and constraints are mapped before anything moves. Risk is taken off the table in the office, not in the field.

  2. 02

    Plan & sequence

    Cut-and-fill, plant and crew are sequenced against the schedule. Every step knows its start and its standard.

  3. 03

    Execute & measure

    Own crews run the machines with machine control, and supervisors check the work against the model every day.

  4. 04

    Close out & hand over

    As-builts, testing and documentation are completed as we go — handover is a signature, not a scramble.
