Who we are — 01

[ 01 / 04 ]

What defines METRIC isn't just the work we do. It's the way we do it.

Metric Civil started with one excavator and a driver who refused to cut corners. Thirteen years on, we take on the jobs that reshape cities — site prep, deep utilities, roads and remediation — with a single rule: plan like the deadline matters, build like the structure outlives us.

  • 01

    Planning

    Every phase is mapped before dirt moves. Survey, model, sequence — then execute.

  • 02

    Execution

    Own crews, in-house plant, and a field team that answers for the result — not the hours.

  • 03

    Technology

    Machine control, drone topo and live as-builts keep survey error out of the ground.

  • 04

    Safety

    Zero-incident is the target on every site. It's the non-negotiable, never the aspiration.
