Life at Metric — 01

[ 01 / 04 ]

People who'd rather get it right.

Metric runs on field judgement. We hire people who read ground, own their calls and would rather do it right once than fast twice. The rest — the machines, the models, the method — we teach.

0% of our superintendents came up through the crew — trained here, promoted here.

What you get — 02

[ 02 / 04 ]
  • 01

    Own the outcome

    You run your scope and answer for it. No committee, no hand-off, no blame when it slips.

  • 02

    Real equipment

    Late-model plant, maintained in-house. You won't nurse a tired machine through a shift.

  • 03

    A schedule you can plan

    Overtime is scheduled, not assumed. Days off are days off. The calendar means something.

  • 04

    Crews that stay

    Most of the field has been here five years or more — and it's the reason the work holds up.
